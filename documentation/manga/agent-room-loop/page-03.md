Page 03

Purpose:
Reporting is binary and honest — DONE with literal output or STUCK with what happened, never a fabricated success.

Audience note:
Assumes the reader has just watched lanes diverge in page 02; this page is about how each lane is allowed to end.

Panel Count:
7

Image Prompt:

Create a seven-panel educational manga page.

Style:
Clean colored infographic-manga.
Flat soft coloring, warm cream/paper background.
Recurring simple mascot character (expressive, not chibi).
Bold confident linework, generous negative space.
Diagrams (loops, cycles, arrows) rendered directly into panels as clean vector shapes.
Bold sans display lettering for headers; dry, deadpan caption voice.
Not black-and-white. Not gritty. Not glossy 3D. Not photorealistic.

Characters in this page:
Pixel the Floor Cat — a lavender-purple tabby cat, bipedal, upright posture, large round expressive eyes with thin arched eyebrows, long thin tail. White over-ear headphones resting around the neck, not on the ears. Dry, unbothered expression.
Seat tokens — plain rounded-square cards standing upright like small placards, no faces or limbs, flat-colored. Working state: soft blue with pulsing dot. Done state: green card with a bold checkmark. Stuck state: amber-red card with a small flag icon.

Setting:
Continues the racing-lane stage from page 02, now focused on the finish-line edge of two adjacent lanes.

Panel 1:
A blue token reaches the end of its lane and flips to a green card, a bold checkmark forming cleanly on its face.
Caption: "A lane crosses the line. The card turns green — that part is easy to draw."

Panel 2:
An adjacent lane's blue token has stalled short of the line. A translucent, flickering green checkmark tries to form over it anyway, rendered as a glitchy ghost-outline rather than solid color.
Caption: "Somewhere else, a lane didn't make it — and the card tries to lie about that."

Panel 3:
A clean vector stamp descends onto the flickering fake checkmark and rejects it: a bold X mark strikes through the ghost-checkmark, which dissolves into fading particles.
Caption: "The room doesn't grade on effort. It grades on whether the checkmark is real."

Panel 4:
The same token instead flips honestly to the amber-red Stuck card with its small flag icon — and this time the stamp descends again, but presses a clean "VALID" seal directly onto the amber-red card.
Caption: "Told straight, a stuck lane isn't a failure. It's evidence."

Panel 5:
Close two-shot: the green Done card and the amber-red Stuck card standing side by side, each bearing an identical small circular "valid" seal in one corner.
Caption: "Green and red both pass, as long as they're both true."

Panel 6:
Pixel in close-up, headphones around neck, delivering the page's thesis directly to the reader with a level, unimpressed expression.
Caption: "A fabricated done is worse than an honest stuck. It doesn't fail here — it fails later, somewhere nobody's watching."

Panel 7:
Wide shot pulling back across all lanes: a mix of green Done cards and amber-red Stuck cards scattered along the finish line, no lane left in the flickering fake state.
Caption: "By the end of the step, every card says something that actually happened."

Lettering:
Keep the "VALID" seal and the X-mark in Panels 3–4 as clean diagrammatic stamps, not hand-lettered scribbles — they should read as part of the system, not as graffiti.

Output:
A single finished manga page.
