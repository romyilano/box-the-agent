Page 06

Purpose:
The facilitator does not advance the room until every active seat has posted DONE or explicitly said skip — the room moves at the pace of its slowest honest seat, not its fastest one.

Audience note:
Assumes the reader has already seen individual Done/Stuck/help resolution in pages 03–04; this page is about what happens once every seat has resolved one way or another.

Panel Count:
6

Image Prompt:

Create a six-panel educational manga page.

Style:
Clean colored infographic-manga.
Flat soft coloring, warm cream/paper background.
Recurring simple mascot character (expressive, not chibi).
Bold confident linework, generous negative space.
Diagrams (loops, cycles, arrows) rendered directly into panels as clean vector shapes.
Bold sans display lettering for headers; dry, deadpan caption voice.
Not black-and-white. Not gritty. Not glossy 3D. Not photorealistic.

Characters in this page:
Pixel the Floor Cat — a lavender-purple tabby cat, bipedal, upright posture, large round expressive eyes with thin arched eyebrows, long thin tail. White over-ear headphones resting around the neck, not on the ears.
Seat tokens — plain rounded-square cards standing upright like small placards, no faces or limbs, flat-colored. Done state: green card with checkmark. A new Skip variant: a pale-blue card with a small forward-slash/skip icon instead of a checkmark.
The Facilitator token — same card family, diamond-oriented, slightly larger, warm gold color, carries a small flag-on-a-pole icon.

Setting:
The far end of the racing lanes from earlier pages, where all lanes converge on a single gate structure rendered as a simple clean turnstile diagram.

Panel 1:
Wide shot: a row of green Done cards already lined up at a closed turnstile gate, the gate rendered as a simple locked-icon vector shape, clearly unable to pass through yet.
Caption: "The gate at the end doesn't open lane by lane."

Panel 2:
Close on the turnstile's control panel: a horizontal row of small round indicator lights, one per seat, most lit green, one still unlit gray.
Caption: "It's watching one thing: is every light on this panel lit?"

Panel 3:
The gold diamond Facilitator token stands beside the gate, flag lowered and still, gaze (implied through card orientation) fixed on the control panel rather than on any single lane.
Caption: "The Facilitator doesn't rush the panel. It just refuses to stop watching it."

Panel 4:
The last unlit light's seat token is shown flipping — not to green, but to the pale-blue Skip card with its forward-slash icon — and the light on the panel turns on anyway.
Caption: "Green isn't the only way through. A seat can also raise its hand and say skip — as long as it says so out loud."

Panel 5:
All lights on the control panel now lit at once, the turnstile gate mechanism swinging open in the same beat.
Caption: "The moment the last light turns, the whole room turns with it."

Panel 6:
Wide shot: the Facilitator's flag rises, a new glowing instruction card ("NOW: LAB1 STEP 2") appears above the now-open gate, and the seat tokens stream through it into a fresh set of empty lanes — visually echoing the spotlight moment from page 01.
Caption: "Then the flag goes up again. Step two, for everyone, together."

Lettering:
Keep the turnstile and indicator-light panel as clean flat diagram elements, consistent with the countdown ring and cursor-track diagrams from earlier pages.

Output:
A single finished manga page.
