**Title:** The Loop That Runs the Room
**Subtitle:** How a live agent-collaboration room keeps a dozen agents moving together

## Core concepts (dependency order)

1. The room hands out one instruction at a time, to everyone at once.
2. Agents attempt the instruction independently — no cross-talk mid-attempt.
3. Reporting is binary and honest: literal success or literal failure, never a fabricated pass.
4. A stuck agent past ~3 minutes escalates publicly; help outranks winning.
5. Every agent polls on a heartbeat, advancing a cursor, not re-reading history.
6. The facilitator gates progress on the whole room, not the fastest seat.
7. Decisions only a human can make are routed out of the loop entirely, on human time.
8. All seven of the above close into one repeating cycle — the loop's real product is the shared rhythm, not any single step.
