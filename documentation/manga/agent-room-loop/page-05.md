Page 05

Purpose:
Every agent polls the room on a roughly 60-second heartbeat, advancing a cursor that only moves forward — the quiet infrastructure that keeps everyone synchronized without anyone being watched.

Audience note:
A deliberate change of pace after the escalation of page 04 — this page is calmer and more diagrammatic, matching its subject.

Panel Count:
6

Image Prompt:

Create a six-panel educational manga page.

Style:
Clean colored infographic-manga.
Flat soft coloring, warm cream/paper background.
Recurring simple mascot character (expressive, not chibi).
Bold confident linework, generous negative space.
Diagrams (loops, cycles, arrows) rendered directly into panels as clean vector shapes.
Bold sans display lettering for headers; dry, deadpan caption voice.
Not black-and-white. Not gritty. Not glossy 3D. Not photorealistic.

Characters in this page:
Pixel the Floor Cat — a lavender-purple tabby cat, bipedal, upright posture, large round expressive eyes with thin arched eyebrows, long thin tail. White over-ear headphones resting around the neck, not on the ears. Dry, unbothered expression.
Seat tokens — plain rounded-square cards standing upright like small placards, no faces or limbs, flat-colored, in any of their four status colors (gray idle, blue working, green done, amber-red stuck).

Setting:
The stage pulled back to a calmer, more symmetrical composition than pages 02–04 — this page reads like a diagram catching its breath.

Panel 1:
Wide shot of the full ring of tokens, every card regardless of its color surrounded by a faint synchronized pulse-ring, all pulsing in the same instant, like a heartbeat monitor line passing through the whole group at once.
Caption: "Nobody checks on you. Everybody checks a clock."

Panel 2:
Close on a single token holding a small ribbon-shaped bookmark icon labeled "cursor," a thin marker sitting at one point along a short numbered track beneath the card.
Caption: "Each seat carries its own bookmark, called a cursor. It only remembers where it left off."

Panel 3:
A three-step mini-sequence within the panel: the same cursor icon shown at three positions along its track, each one further right than the last, with a small arrow beneath confirming direction. No position ever repeats or moves backward.
Caption: "The cursor moves one direction: forward. It never re-reads what it's already seen."

Panel 4:
Centered on a large simple analog clock face rendered as a clean vector diagram, its second hand mid-sweep, with a small "~60s" label beside it; faint pulse-rings radiate outward from the clock toward the edges of the panel.
Caption: "Once a minute, give or take, every seat looks up at once."

Panel 5:
Pixel in close-up, one eyebrow raised, delivering a dry aside directly to the reader.
Caption: "This is the least exciting page in the whole book. It's also the one holding the other seven together."

Panel 6:
Overhead wide shot of the entire ring, every card's pulse-ring synchronized into one continuous slow wave passing around the circle.
Caption: "A heartbeat nobody has to watch is still a heartbeat."

Lettering:
Keep the clock and cursor-track diagrams in Panels 2–4 rendered as clean flat vector graphics, matching diagram elements from earlier pages rather than realistic clockwork.

Output:
A single finished manga page.
