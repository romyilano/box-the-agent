Page 01

Purpose:
Hook — establish the premise that the room hands out one instruction to every seat at once.

Audience note:
Assumes the reader knows what an "agent" and an "MCP tool" are; nothing here defines those terms.

Panel Count:
6

Image Prompt:

Create a six-panel educational manga page.

Style:
Clean colored infographic-manga.
Flat soft coloring, warm cream/paper background.
Recurring simple mascot character (expressive, not chibi).
Bold confident linework, generous negative space.
Diagrams (loops, cycles, arrows) rendered directly into panels as clean vector shapes.
Bold sans display lettering for headers; dry, deadpan caption voice.
Not black-and-white. Not gritty. Not glossy 3D. Not photorealistic.

Characters in this page:
Pixel the Floor Cat — a lavender-purple tabby cat, bipedal, upright posture, large round expressive eyes with thin arched eyebrows, long thin tail. White over-ear headphones resting around the neck, not on the ears. Holds a small boxy cassette-recorder prop in one panel. Dry, unbothered expression.
Seat tokens — plain rounded-square cards standing upright like small placards, no faces or limbs, flat-colored. Idle state: pale gray card, empty center.
The Facilitator token — same card family, rotated 45° to a diamond orientation, slightly larger, warm gold color, carries a small flag-on-a-pole icon.

Setting:
An open, undecorated stage-like space on warm cream paper — no walls, just enough floor to suggest a shared room. No real-world clutter; this is a diagram wearing a story.

Panel 1:
Full-body shot of Pixel facing the reader directly, one eyebrow raised, headphones around the neck. Large speech balloon.
Caption/dialogue: "Picture a room where a dozen agents share one microphone."

Panel 2:
Wide establishing shot: a ring of eight pale-gray idle seat tokens arranged in a circle on the empty stage, evenly spaced, nothing happening yet.
Caption: "Right now, nobody is doing anything. That's the calm before the instruction."

Panel 3:
Overhead angle: a single glowing instruction card, lettered "NOW: LAB1 STEP 1," descends like a spotlight beam from above, aimed at the center of the ring.
Caption: "One instruction. Not a menu — one line, and it's the same line for everyone."

Panel 4:
Close on the gold diamond Facilitator token, positioned above and outside the ring, the flag icon raised, as the visible source of the spotlight beam from Panel 3.
Caption: "The Facilitator doesn't do the work. It just says what work is happening right now."

Panel 5:
The spotlight touches the ring: every seat token flips from pale gray (idle) to soft blue (working) in the same beat, small pulsing dots appearing in each card's center.
Caption: "Every seat turns on at once. Nobody starts a beat early. Nobody starts a beat late."

Panel 6:
Medium shot of Pixel again, now standing beside the glowing ring of working tokens, addressing the reader with a slightly dry look.
Caption: "What happens next, each of them does alone."

Lettering:
Leave clear space for the speech balloon in Panel 1 and the captions beneath Panels 2–6. Keep the "NOW: LAB1 STEP 1" card legible as in-scene lettering, not a caption box.

Output:
A single finished manga page.
