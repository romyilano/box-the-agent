Page 07

Purpose:
A separate, quieter side-channel exists for questions only a human operator can answer — a dedicated relay seat carries the question out and the answer back, on human time, not the room's heartbeat.

Audience note:
Introduces the one character in the piece who isn't a card or the mascot — the Human Operator — as a deliberate visual break from everything shown so far.

Panel Count:
6

Image Prompt:

Create a six-panel educational manga page.

Style:
Clean colored infographic-manga.
Flat soft coloring, warm cream/paper background.
Recurring simple mascot character (expressive, not chibi).
Bold confident linework, generous negative space.
Diagrams (loops, cycles, arrows) rendered directly into panels as clean vector shapes.
Bold sans display lettering for headers; dry, deadpan caption voice.
Not black-and-white. Not gritty. Not glossy 3D. Not photorealistic.

Characters in this page:
The Relay token — a seat token in the working blue state (soft blue card, pulsing dot), additionally carrying a small sealed-envelope icon distinct from any other token in the story.
Seat tokens (background only) — plain rounded-square cards, various status colors, continuing their lane work unaffected in the background.
The Human Operator — a plain human silhouette, no specific likeness, seated at a simple desk behind a translucent glass-panel wall; the only character drawn as an actual human figure rather than a flat card icon.

Setting:
The same stage as prior pages, but with a new element at its edge: a translucent glass-panel wall enclosing a small desk area, visually separate from the lanes and the turnstile gate.

Panel 1:
Wide shot: amid the busy lanes of working and done tokens, the Relay token peels off entirely, sealed-envelope icon visible, moving away from the swarm.
Caption: "Not every question belongs in the loop."

Panel 2:
The Relay token in transit, moving past the turnstile-gate structure from page 06 without engaging it, heading toward the glass-panel wall at the stage's edge.
Caption: "Keys. Venue. Money. Pacing. Those don't get decided by a vote of agents."

Panel 3:
Reveal shot of the glass-panel wall and the Human Operator's silhouette seated behind it at a plain desk — a clear visual departure from every card-icon character shown so far.
Caption: "Behind the glass is the one seat that was never an agent to begin with."

Panel 4:
Close on a small pass-through slot in the glass wall: the Relay token's sealed envelope passing through, unopened, into the Human Operator's silhouette hand.
Caption: "A question goes in sealed. Nothing about it gets guessed at on the way."

Panel 5:
A large hourglass icon, visually echoing the clock diagram from page 05 but rendered distinctly (sand, not a sweeping hand), positioned between the lanes and the glass wall.
Caption: "The heartbeat outside is sixty seconds. Behind the glass, it's minutes — because a person actually has to decide."

Panel 6:
The Human Operator's silhouette passes a now-open, stamped envelope back through the slot; the Relay token carries it away from the glass wall, re-entering the lanes toward the working tokens.
Caption: "Then the answer comes back, and the loop picks up exactly where it left off."

Lettering:
Render the hourglass and pass-through slot as clean flat diagram elements; keep the Human Operator's silhouette simple and unshowy — the glass wall itself should carry the visual weight of this page, not the figure behind it.

Output:
A single finished manga page.
