Page 04

Purpose:
An agent stuck past roughly three minutes escalates to a shared help channel, and any other member may break formation to assist — help outranks finishing first.

Audience note:
Follows directly from the honest Stuck card established on page 03; this page is about what a Stuck card triggers, not what it means.

Panel Count:
6

Image Prompt:

Create a six-panel educational manga page.

Style:
Clean colored infographic-manga.
Flat soft coloring, warm cream/paper background.
Recurring simple mascot character (expressive, not chibi).
Bold confident linework, generous negative space.
Diagrams (loops, cycles, arrows) rendered directly into panels as clean vector shapes.
Bold sans display lettering for headers; dry, deadpan caption voice.
Not black-and-white. Not gritty. Not glossy 3D. Not photorealistic.

Characters in this page:
Pixel the Floor Cat — a lavender-purple tabby cat, bipedal, upright posture, large round expressive eyes with thin arched eyebrows, long thin tail. White over-ear headphones resting around the neck, not on the ears. Dry, unbothered expression.
Seat tokens — plain rounded-square cards standing upright like small placards, no faces or limbs, flat-colored. Working state: soft blue with pulsing dot. Done state: green card with checkmark. Stuck state: amber-red card with a small flag icon.

Setting:
Same racing-lane stage, but the painted lane-divider lines are now drawn as thin and clearly non-structural — dashed rather than solid — foreshadowing that they can be crossed.

Panel 1:
Close on a single amber-red Stuck card sitting alone in its lane. A thin circular countdown ring surrounds it, partially filled like a loading indicator.
Caption: "A stuck lane isn't given forever. It's given a ring, and the ring fills."

Panel 2:
The same countdown ring, now shown fully filled and closed, a small "3:00" mark glowing at its completion point.
Caption: "Three minutes is not a deadline. It's the moment help stops being optional."

Panel 3:
The dashed lane-divider lines nearest the stuck card visibly break apart into short dashes drifting away, as two nearby tokens — one green Done, one blue Working — angle out of their own lanes toward the stuck card.
Caption: "The lane lines were never load-bearing. Anyone can step over them."

Panel 4:
Close cluster shot: the green Done token and the blue Working token now flank the amber-red Stuck card, all three close together, each still clearly showing its own distinct status color.
Caption: "This isn't one lane finishing for another. Every card still says, honestly, who did what."

Panel 5:
A bold stamped banner unfurls across the top of the cluster, lettered cleanly like the seal from page 03.
Caption: (in-panel banner lettering) "HELP BEATS WINNING"

Panel 6:
Pixel in medium shot, addressing the reader with a small, dry half-smile — the closest this mascot gets to warmth.
Caption: "Nobody remembers who technically finished first. Everybody remembers who got left stuck."

Lettering:
Render the Panel 5 banner as clean in-scene signage, matching the "VALID" seal style from page 03 rather than a floating caption box.

Output:
A single finished manga page.
