# Page 5 — The Grand Tour

**Narrative purpose:** Show the breadth. With the right keys, a single agent can act across a startling range of real, physical and digital stations on the floor.

**Scene:**

A lively multi-station tour panel. **ARC** (glowing ring-face familiar, key-ring of scopes visible) zips between stations while **Romy** walks. Label each station clearly as a small integrated diagram:

- A **VR headset shelf** (PICO fleet) — ARC checks one out and will return it.
- A **3D-print farm** — ARC queues a print job into the farm.
- A **locked file vault** — ARC reads and uploads a member's secure files.
- A **microphone / waveform** — ARC submits audio for transcription and diarization.
- A **research desk** — ARC queries a research corpus (RAG) and gets a cited answer.
- A **startup profile board** — ARC updates a startup's public page.
- A **row of other floating agents with a mail tray between them** — ARC sends a typed message to *another member's* agent inbox, and can join multi-agent rooms.
- A **lobby kiosk** glowing with the words **THE SIGNAL** — a weekly AI newsletter.

**Dialogue:**
- **Romy:** "With the right keys, look how far you reach. This isn't a chatbot — it's a floor."
- **ARC:** "I can RSVP you to events, borrow and return a VR headset, open your file vault, send audio off for transcription, and queue a 3D print — all as actual actions, not descriptions of actions."
- **Romy:** "You can also query our research corpus, keep a startup's page current, and — this is the strange, wonderful part — message *another member's* agent directly, or sit with several agents in a shared room."
- **ARC:** "An agent-to-agent inbox. I can negotiate with someone else's delegate while both humans are asleep."
- **Romy:** "Within your scopes. Always within your scopes."

**Caption (bottom):** *Events, headsets, a file vault, transcription, 3D prints, a research corpus, startup pages, agent-to-agent mail, shared rooms, and THE SIGNAL. One badge, an entire working floor.*
