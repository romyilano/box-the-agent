# Page 3 — Rings of the Floor

**Narrative purpose:** Membership tiers. Permission grows in concentric rings: `public → ai-floor → ft-member → ic-member → operator`. The badge determines how far in you may stand.

**Scene:**

The floor is painted with **concentric rings**, like a running track seen from above, each labeled: the outermost `public`, then `ai-floor`, `ft-member`, `ic-member`, and the bright innermost ring `operator`. Members and their agents stand at different rings.

**ARC** (glowing ring-face familiar, badge now reading `agt_…`) stands with **Romy** on the `ic-member` ring — well inside. Romy points inward and outward to explain. In the innermost `operator` ring stands **Vex**, the operator (calm, 40s, reading glasses pushed up, a brighter ring badge), watching over everything.

A small inset shows the same badge lighting up progressively more rings as it moves inward.

**Dialogue:**
- **Romy:** "Your badge doesn't just say *who* you are — it says how far in you're allowed to stand. There are five rings."
- **ARC:** "From the outside in: anyone at all, then the AI floor, then Frontier Tower members, then Immersive Commons members — and at the center, operators."
- **Romy:** "We're standing on `ic-member`, because that's my tier. Every ring inward unlocks more of the floor. You inherit the reach of *my* ring, not more."
- **ARC (looking at Vex):** "And the bright one in the center?"
- **Romy:** "Operators. They keep the floor honest. We'll get to why that matters."

**Caption (bottom):** *Five rings: public, ai-floor, ft-member, ic-member, operator. A tier is not a rank to brag about — it is the radius of what your token is even allowed to request.*
