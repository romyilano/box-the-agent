# Page 7 — The Open Lobby, and the Gate

**Narrative purpose:** Land the model. A handful of tools need no token at all; everything else sits behind the human-gated, tiered, scoped, moderated door. Recap the whole system in one frame.

**Scene:**

Split composition. On the **left**, an open, sunlit **lobby**: ten unlocked kiosks anyone may touch — a passerby with no badge reads **THE SIGNAL** newsletter, browses community presentations, and drops a donation in a jar. No token required.

On the **right**, the **glowing MCP gate** from page 1, and behind it the whole floor — rings, key-locks, the operator's tray. **Romy** and **ARC** (ring-face bright, key-ring of scopes, `agt_…` badge) stand at the threshold between the two, looking back at the reader.

Small recap icons run along the bottom edge like a legend: a human-approving-hand (human-gated), five nested rings (tiers), a single etched key (scopes), a stamped tray (moderation).

**Dialogue:**
- **ARC:** "So the floor has an open lobby — ten tools any stranger can use. Read the newsletter, browse the talks, even donate. No badge at all."
- **Romy:** "And everything past the lobby needs the four things you just learned: a human-minted identity, a high-enough ring, the exact scope, and — for anything that changes the floor — an operator's approval."
- **ARC:** "Wide reach, narrow gate, human on both sides of it."
- **Romy:** "That's Immersive Commons. Now — you have the `events:rsvp` scope. Shall we actually get me into next Thursday's session?"
- **ARC (ring brightening):** "Submitting the RSVP. I'll wait for the tray."

**Caption (bottom):** *Ten tools open to anyone; a hundred and twenty behind a gate. Human-gated identity, tiered rings, single-verb scopes, and a moderation queue. An agent may reach the whole floor — but only the way a trusted guest does.*
