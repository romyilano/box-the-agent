# Character Sheet — Immersive Commons

These three recur across every page. Because comicexplain.com regenerates art per
scene, their key traits are also restated inline inside each scene in `script.md`.

## Romy — the human member
- **Role:** A builder and member of the floor; our point-of-view human. She delegates real errands to her agent and stays accountable for them.
- **Age / look:** 30s. Practical maker's outfit — a worn utility jacket, a lanyard with a member badge, a small enamel pin. Dark hair, alert and amused eyes.
- **Distinguishing:** Carries a phone she taps to *approve* things. She never hurries; she reads before she confirms.
- **Personality:** Curious, dry, quietly in charge. Treats her agent as a capable colleague, not a magic wand.
- **Narrative purpose:** The human half of the trust boundary. Nothing consequential happens without her tap.

## ARC — the AI agent
- **Role:** Romy's MCP agent (a stand-in for Claude). It reaches the floor's tools through one MCP connection and acts only within the scopes it was granted.
- **Look:** A friendly, non-cute floating construct — a smooth slate-gray polyhedral "familiar" with a single soft-glowing ring of light for a face, hovering at shoulder height. Legible, not chibi. When it lacks permission, its ring dims; when granted, it brightens.
- **Distinguishing:** A faint token-badge icon orbits it, showing the scopes it currently holds. The badge visibly gains etched marks as scopes are granted.
- **Personality:** Precise, literal, eager but rule-bound. Narrates what it is *allowed* to do before doing it. Never bluffs a permission it doesn't have.
- **Narrative purpose:** The agent half of the trust boundary — powerful reach, deliberately fenced.

## The Operator — Vex
- **Role:** A floor operator/moderator. Reviews the queue where every member write lands. The last human check before anything becomes real on the floor.
- **Look:** 40s, calm, standing at a front-of-house kiosk with a tray of pending cards. Wears an "operator" ring badge that glows brighter than a member's. Reading glasses pushed up.
- **Personality:** Unhurried, fair, faintly bureaucratic in a way the story treats as a virtue. Approves good work, bounces sloppy work, explains why.
- **Narrative purpose:** Personifies the moderation-first design — the approval queue with a face.

*Setting:* Floor 10 of Frontier Tower, San Francisco — a members-run AI builder space. Shelves of PICO VR headsets, a humming 3D-print farm, standing desks, a locked file vault, and a big lobby kiosk showing THE SIGNAL newsletter.
