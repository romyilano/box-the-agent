# Page 1 — One Door Into a Real Place

**Narrative purpose:** Hook. Establish that this is a *physical* floor an agent can act upon, reached through a single MCP door.

**Scene (for comicexplain.com — write the art from this):**

A wide establishing panel of Floor 10 of Frontier Tower in San Francisco: a members-run AI builder space. Standing desks, a shelf of VR headsets, a humming 3D-print farm in the corner, a big lobby kiosk screen. Clean black-and-white educational-manga look, light gray screentone, legible.

**Romy** (30s, worn utility jacket, member lanyard, dark hair, dry amused eyes) stands in the middle of the floor holding her phone. Beside her floats **ARC**, her AI agent: a smooth slate-gray polyhedral familiar with a single soft-glowing ring of light for a face, hovering at shoulder height, a faint token-badge icon orbiting it.

At the entrance stands a single glowing doorway, labeled in clean lettering: **MCP**. Through it, we glimpse the whole floor's machinery as floating tool-icons.

**Dialogue:**
- **ARC:** "So this is a real place — desks, headsets, a print farm — and you want me to *do things* here on your behalf?"
- **Romy:** "That's the idea. Everything on this floor is reachable through one door: an MCP server. You connect once, and behind it are a hundred and thirty tools."
- **ARC:** "One connection, the entire floor. What stops me from simply walking through and touching all of it?"
- **Romy (small smile):** "That's the whole story. Let me show you the door before you assume it's open."

**Caption (bottom):** *Immersive Commons, Floor 10. One MCP door. A hundred and thirty tools behind it. None of them free.*
